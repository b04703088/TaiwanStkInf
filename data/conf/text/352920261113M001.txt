[p.12]
  未來展望 – 新IP技術&商務平台擴展
  ◼  New IP Technologies
  1.  Advanced-Node OTP and PUF-based Hardware Security: Continuing to develop and qualify next-generation OTP and PUF-based
  hardware security solutions for 2nm GAA and sub-2nm nodes, addressing growing customer requirements for device identity, key protection,
  Secure Boot, and hardware Root of Trust.
  2.  Next-Generation 1T Flash: Our 1T logic technology continues to advance across both embedded and standalone memory applications. Its
  logic-process-compatible architecture offers greater scalability, simplified process integration, and enhanced cost efficiency, making it an
  attractive solution for future AI, industrial, and edge computing applications.
  ◼  Business Development Platforms
  1.  Data Center and AI Compute Security Platform: Accelerating adoption of Caliptra across CPU, AI accelerator, and AI ASIC ecosystems is
  driving strong demand for our security solutions, ranging from foundational Hardware Root of Trust IP to Caliptra-compatible Security
  Subsystems and turnkey integration services. As AI infrastructure continues to scale, trusted computing is becoming a fundamental
  requirement across next-generation silicon platforms.
  2.  Post-Quantum Security Platform: AI makes software and firmware cheaper to attack. Arriving quantum computing can further challenge
  existing cryptographic engines. We continue to strengthen our PUF-PQC portfolio with attack-resistant hardware security technologies,
  including side-channel protection, to support customers' transition to the post-quantum era and emerging security standards.
  3.  Chiplet Security Platform: Together with ecosystem partners, we are building an end-to-end security framework for chiplet-based systems,
  covering supply-chain traceability, identity and authentication, secure provisioning, die-to-die communication, key management, and
  Hardware Root of Trust. These capabilities help address the growing security challenges arising from AI computing, advanced packaging,
  heterogeneous integration, and today's evolving geopolitical landscape.
  4.  HSM Edge Server and SECaaS Platform: The PUF-based HSM Edge Server combines device identity, key and certificate management,
  secure OTA updates, signature verification, privacy protection, and Post-Quantum Cryptography migration. Early opportunities are
  progressing in Root CA, enterprise AI token-exchange signing, DID wallets, as well as automotive OTA, industrial control, Edge AI, and data
  center applications.
